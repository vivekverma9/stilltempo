# StillTempo — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 October 2026

StillTempo is a Chrome extension that transforms your browser's New Tab page into a personalised space combining Workspace and Lounge, productivity tools, curated photography, weather, humour and optional internet radio.

We believe your New Tab should be yours. This policy explains what information the extension handles and where it goes.

## 1. Information stored on your device

StillTempo uses Chrome local extension storage to remember your Hitlist tasks and completion status, custom shortcuts and their URLs, focus timer settings and session state, appearance and mode preferences, wallpaper selections and saved photos, weather preferences (including selected city or approximate location and cached results), radio preferences and other settings.

These are stored in your browser profile. StillTempo does not require an account and does not send your tasks or saved shortcuts to a StillTempo account or central database.

You can export and import a JSON backup. Exported files stay wherever you choose to save them.

## 2. Weather and location

Weather is enabled by default. When automatic approximate location is enabled and no city has been selected manually, StillTempo contacts GeoJS to estimate your location based on your network IP address. Approximate coordinates are used to request forecasts from Open-Meteo. Manual city searches send the entered city name to Open-Meteo's geocoding service.

You can disable weather or automatic approximate location, or select a city yourself. StillTempo does not request GPS permissions or precise device location.

## 3. External services

Certain features contact third-party services:

- Cloudflare and Unsplash — curated wallpapers, hosted photographs and Unsplash image-download tracking.
- GeoJS — IP-based approximate location for weather.
- Open-Meteo — weather forecasts and city search.
- icanhazdadjoke — optional humour.
- Internet radio providers, including COTN Radio and RelaxingJazz and designated fallback broadcasters — audio when you start playback.
- Your browser's default search provider — searches you submit.

These providers may receive your IP address, requested resources and standard connection metadata, subject to their own privacy practices. StillTempo does not transmit your locally stored Hitlist or shortcuts to them.

## 4. How information is used

Information is handled to run the New Tab page, remember preferences, maintain focus sessions and provide requested content. The extension package does not contain a dedicated advertising or behavioural analytics system. StillTempo's operator does not sell user data or use it for targeted advertising, creditworthiness assessment or lending.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains in Chrome's extension storage until changed, cleared or removed through supported browser controls. Content such as forecasts, wallpapers, approximate location and humour may be cached. Disabling a feature stops further feature-related requests but may leave existing caches.

Uninstalling StillTempo removes its extension-managed browser storage according to Chrome's normal lifecycle. Exported JSON backups must be deleted separately. Third-party providers may retain request information under their own policies.

## 6. Permissions and security

StillTempo uses Chrome permissions for storage, browser-default search, focus scheduling, favicon display, optional notifications and audio. It does not request access to complete browsing history, arbitrary website contents, saved passwords or personal communications. External network connections use HTTPS and executable JavaScript is bundled in the extension, not fetched and executed remotely.

## 7. Your choices

Settings let you customise Workspace and Lounge, disable weather, approximate location, online humour, radio playback and desktop notifications, and change your saved preferences. You can export data or uninstall the extension.

## 8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in features, providers or data practices. Material changes will appear in the published policy and, where required, in extension communications.
